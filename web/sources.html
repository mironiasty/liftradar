Data sources

Snow and resort status

Tirol Snow Card snowcard.tirol.at
Daily reports of the Tirol resorts (snow depth, lifts, slopes, open/closed), as the resorts submit them to iSKI.
SalzburgerLand snow report salzburgerland.com
The same kind of report for the Salzburg resorts, from the regional tourism board (also Feuerkogel).
Kärnten snow report kaernten.at
The same kind of report for the Carinthian resorts, from Kärnten Werbung, the regional tourism board.
Schladming-Dachstein schladming-dachstein.at
The tourism board's page of each area's lifts and slopes with their state, for the Schladming 4-Berge-Skischaukel (Planai, Hochwurzen, Hauser Kaibling and Reiteralm, added up), Galsterberg and Ramsau am Dachstein (Rittisberg). No report time.
Ski amadé skiamade.com
Every lift and slope with its state, for Hochkönig (Maria Alm, Dienten and Mühlbach, added up), Shuttleberg Flachauwinkl - Kleinarl and Großarltal - Dorfgastein: they say what's open, the snow comes from the SalzburgerLand report. No report time.
Resorts' own websites
Snow measuring stations of Hintertuxer Gletscher, Sölden, Kitzsteinhorn, Kaunertaler Gletscher and Pitztaler Gletscher, used instead of their regional report, and the date of the last snowfall at Hintertux and Sölden. Every lift and slope with its state for Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau, St. Johann in Tirol, Wildkogel, Nauders, Katschberg, Grosseck-Speiereck and Bad Kleinkirchheim (with its snow depth), which say what's open while their regional report isn't up to date. No report time.
Ski Arlberg skiarlberg.at
Every lift and slope of St. Anton, Lech Zürs and Warth-Schröcken, and the snow depth of St. Anton and Lech Zürs, from the lift companies' live info.
infosnow (APG|SGA) infosnow.ch
Live status of Swiss ski areas, as the lift companies publish it: every lift and slope with its type, difficulty and length, plus snow depths where the resort enters them.
Titlis (SISMEDIA) titlis.ch
Engelberg-Titlis' status export (Siscontrol's SISMEDIA), which its website reads: every lift and slope with its state and type, and slope lengths. No report time.
Jungfraubahnen jungfrau.ch
Live data of the Jungfrau Ski Region's three areas (Grindelwald-Wengen, Grindelwald-First, Mürren-Schilthorn): every ski lift and slope with its state, the update time, and snow on the peak and in the valley.
Aletsch Arena aletscharena.ch
The winter status report: every lift and slope with its state and type, and the update time.
Airolo Pesciüm airolo.ch
The lift company's status page: when it says the area is closed for the season, Airolo reads closed rather than “no report” while its infosnow entry isn't updated. Lifts and slopes are infosnow's.
Davos Klosters davos.ch
The tourism board's lift and slope report, by mountain (Parsenn, Jakobshorn, Madrisa, Rinerhorn, Schatzalp): every lift and slope with its state, slope lengths, and snow depths at Weissfluhjoch and Jakobshorn. No report time.
SLF, WSL Institute for Snow and Avalanche Research slf.ch
Measured snow depth and new snow at the stations nearest to Swiss resorts that don't report depths (automatic IMIS stations and observers' study plots), CC BY 4.0.
Open Data Hub South Tyrol opendatahub.com
Live status of every lift and slope of the Dolomiti Superski areas, as Dolomiti Superski publishes it (CC0), and the South Tyrol snow report (lifts, slopes, snow measuring points) from the province's tourism database.
Valle d'Aosta snow bulletin lovevda.it
Every lift and slope of the Aosta Valley areas (and Alagna), with snow depth, from the regional tourism board.
Trentino snow bulletin visittrentino.info
Lifts, slope km, snow depth and last snowfall of the Trentino areas, from the provincial tourism board.
Valtellina di Bormio bormio.eu
Every lift and slope of Bormio (with San Colombano), Santa Caterina and the Stelvio glacier, from the tourism board.
Livigno livigno.eu
Lift status of Livigno's three lift companies, from the tourism board.
Riserva Bianca, Aprica, Monte Pora riservabianca.it apricaonline.com montepora.com
The resorts' own status pages: every lift and slope with its state (and, for Aprica and Riserva Bianca, each slope's length). No report time.
Campo Felice, Abetone, Ovindoli campofelice.it multipassabetone.it ovindolimagnola.it
The resorts' own status pages: every lift and slope with its state (Campo Felice and Abetone, with each slope's length), or only how many are open (Ovindoli). No report time.
Monte Cimone, Mondolè Ski cimonesci.it pratonevoso.com
The resorts' own status pages, with the report's time: every lift and slope with its state, for Monte Cimone and for Mondolè Ski's two areas, Prato Nevoso and Frabosa Soprana.
EtnaSci etnasci.it
The snow report of the Etna's ski school, kept by its instructors: every lift of Etna Nord (Piano Provenzana) with its state, the snow on the slopes, and the day it was updated.
Italian resorts' own live lists roccaraso.net skiareavalchiavenna.it courmayeur-montblanc.com skiworldahrntal.it merano-suedtirol.it artesina.it pianidibobbio.com san-domenico.com alpedimera.it manivaski.it montealpet.com matese.ski nuovalizzola.it sciareapescasseroli.it doganaccia2000.it cornoallescale.org ilgransasso.it skilagorai.it
The status pages of resorts whose regional report is out of date before the season, or that have none: Roccaraso, Madesimo, Courmayeur, Klausberg, Schwemmalm (Meran's tourism board), Artesina, Piani di Bobbio, San Domenico, Alpe di Mera, Maniva, Monte Alpet, Campitello Matese, Lizzola, Pescasseroli, Doganaccia, Corno alle Scale and Campo Imperatore. Every lift (and, except Monte Alpet and Lizzola, every slope) with its state, which decides whether the resort is open; snow and the area's size stay the regional report's. Only Maniva, Alpe di Mera and Courmayeur give the report's time. SkiLagorai: only how many lifts and slopes are open.
Kudy z nudy (CzechTourism) kudyznudy.cz
The snow report of the Czech state tourism agency, from each ski area's page: every lift and slope with its type, difficulty and length, snow depth on the slopes and new snow, as the resorts enter them. No report time.
Czech ski areas' own pages skiresort.cz novakojachymov.cz
Velká Úpa from its operator's (SkiResort Černá hora – Pec) lift and slope tables: every lift and slope with its state, type or colour and length, no report time. Jáchymov from the counters on its home page: how many lifts and slopes are open, no report time.
Polskie Koleje Linowe (PKL) pkl.pl
Every lift and slope of Kasprowy Wierch, Jaworzyna Krynicka, Palenica and Mosorny Groń, with snow cover, from the lift company.
Zieleniec zieleniec.pl
Every lift and slope of Zieleniec, with snow cover, from Zieleniec Sport Arena. The site refuses the fetcher's servers for now, so only the area's size is shown.
Tatry Mountain Resorts (TMR) jasna.sk
The lift and slope tables TMR's sites embed, for Jasná, Tatranská Lomnica, Štrbské Pleso, Starý Smokovec and Szczyrk: every lift and slope with its state, type or difficulty and length. No report time. Outside the season the lift tables list only the summer lifts, so they're used only while a slope is open.
Slovak ski areas' own pages
Every lift and slope with its state on the sites of Park Snow Donovaly, Kubínska hoľa (with snow depth and report time), Veľká Rača (report time), Malinô Brdo and Orava Snow (snow depth); how many lifts and slopes are open on Vrátna's and Ski Telgárt's home pages. The others have no report time.
Beskid Sport Arena beskidsportarena.pl
The tables of its lifts and slopes on its own site, each with its state. No report time.
Tylicz.ski tylicz.ski
The states of its lifts and slopes on its home page. No report time; its slope km from OpenSkiMap.
Kotelnica Białczańska bialkatatrzanska.pl
The state of every lift and slope its conditions page shows (Bania, Kaniówka and Horników Wierch with it), with the snow cover on each slope. No report time.
Tatry Super Ski tatrysuperski.pl
The conditions widget of the Tatry Super Ski pass that its stations show on their sites, for Grapa, Jurgów, Rusiń-Ski, Koziniec, Czorsztyn-Ski, Harenda, Ski Suche and, in Slovakia, Bachledka, Strednica, Strachan and Oravice: every lift and slope with its state, and the slopes' snow cover. No report time.
Polish ski areas' own pages
The lift and slope states on the sites of Czarna Góra, Karpacz Ski Arena, Ski Arena Szrenica, Pilsko, Wierchomla, Laskowa-Kamionna, Czarny Groń, Cieńków, Soszów, Nowa Osada, Kiczera Ski, Czarnów-Ski and Szwajcaria Bałtowska. Only Czarna Góra and Karpacz date their conditions. Some list only their slopes; their lift totals and, where the page gives no lengths, their slope km are the area's size.
Lumiplan snow bulletins lumiplan.com
The daily report French resorts enter in Lumiplan, from each station's bulletin: snow depth, snow quality and the last snowfall at the resort's measuring points, and every lift and slope with its state. No report time.
Montgenèvre montgenevre.com
The resort's Infos Neige page, which shows Lumiplan's data (digisnow): every lift and slope of Montgenèvre's own sector with its state, and of the Italian Via Lattea's (Sestriere, Sauze d'Oulx, Sansicario, Claviere), whose own site refuses automated requests. No report time.
ATEN Altitude live.aten-altitude.com
Alpe d'Huez's live page: every lift and slope with its state, and snow depth, snow quality and the last snowfall at its measuring points (before the season, those with snow in the last 30 days). No report time.
Altiservice altiservice.com
The Info pistes pages of Saint-Lary and Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000, from their operator: every lift and slope with its state. Lifts and slopes only, no report time; the snow from Météo-France.
Les Orres lesorres.com
The resort's Infos neige & ouverture des pistes page: every lift and slope of its sectors with its state. Lifts and slopes only, no report time.
La Rosière larosiere.ski
The operator's Ouvertures page: every lift and slope of La Rosière's side of the Espace San Bernardo with its state. Lifts and slopes only, no report time.
Ubaye ubaye.com
The tourist office's snow report for Sainte-Anne-la-Condamine: every lift and slope with its state, and when it was last updated. Lifts and slopes only.
Gresse-en-Vercors gresse-en-vercors.fr
The commune's table of its lifts and slopes, kept by hand, each open or closed. Lifts and slopes only, no report time.
Suomen Hiihtokeskusyhdistys ski.fi
The Finnish Ski Area Association's resort pages: the block each resort fills in (open slopes and lifts, and the day it was updated), the area's slopes by colour and lifts by type, and the list flagging each resort open or closed. No snow depth and no report time.
Finnish resorts' own pages
Open/total slopes and lifts on the sites of Ylläs, Tahko, Himos, Salla, Ukkohalla, Ounasvaara, Riihivuori, Suomutunturi, Kasurila and Teijo, and every slope (and lift) with its state on Vuokatti's, Laajis' and Messilä's. No report time.
Panoraama (HS-visual Art) panoraama.com
The slope maps' live data: Ruka's open slopes and lifts and natural snow depth, and every slope and lift of Iso-Syöte with its state (no report time); every slope and lift of Pyhä, Luosto and Pikku-Syöte with its state and colour, and the time the map was updated.
Finnish Meteorological Institute (FMI) en.ilmatieteenlaitos.fi
Measured snow depth at the weather station nearest to each Finnish resort, CC BY 4.0.
Fnugg (Norske Alpinanlegg og Fjelldestinasjoner) fnugg.no
The Norwegian ski resorts' association's service: every lift and slope of a resort with its state, and the report the resort enters (snow depth, new snow, conditions, dated). No slope lengths. Snow and lift data provided by fnugg; its weather, from Yr, isn't used.
Skimore skimore.no
The status pages of Skimore's Oslo - Tryvann, Kongsberg and Drammen (not on fnugg): every lift and slope with its state, slopes with their colour. No report time, no snow depth.
Pirineu365 (FGC) pirineu365.cat
The Catalan government's railway company's report of La Molina, Vall de Núria, Vallter, Espot, Port Ainé and Boí Taüll: every lift and slope with its state, snow min/max and quality. No report time.
Spanish resorts' own sites
Every lift and slope with its state on the report pages of Valdezcaray, Manzaneda, Valgrande-Pajares, Fuentes de Invierno, Valdesquí and Alto Campoo (with a report time), and in the data the report pages of Baqueira Beret, Sierra Nevada (report time, snow depth) and Astún-Candanchú (each resort's report time, snow depth) read; Formigal-Panticosa from its slope map. The Diputación de León's San Isidro and Leitariegos: every lift and slope with its state, dated by the day. Masella: how many slopes and lifts are open, and its snow depth. Aramón's Cerler, Javalambre and Valdelinares: only whether the resort is closed, and when the report was issued. Puerto de Navacerrada, La Covatilla and Tavascán are listed with their size only, from the operators' sites and skiresort.com.
Slovenian resorts' own sites
Every lift and slope with its state on the sites of Krvavec (with its snow depth and report time), Mariborsko Pohorje, Golte and Stari vrh (snow depth, report time), and the report Soriška planina and Gače show on theirs (state for each day of the week, snow, report time); open/total lifts and slopes on Kranjska Gora's, every lift on Vogel's, and every lift and slope with its state, the snow depth and the time of the last edit on Cerkno's. Rogla, Kope, Velika planina and Ribniško Pohorje are listed with their size only, from the operators' sites and skiresort.com. Snežni telefon, where most resorts enter their report, is a commercial portal and isn't used.
Grandvalira Resorts grandvalira.com ordinoarcalis.com palarinsal.com
The "Estat de pistes" page of each Andorran resort: every lift and slope of each sector with its state, the snow depth range and the time it was updated.
Wintersport-Arena Sauerland wintersport-arena.de
The lift overview of the Sauerland's lift operators: lifts open/total, one snow depth and an open/closed icon for each area. No report time, no slopes or km. Other German resorts are read from their slope maps or their operators' pages, or have no source yet.
Oberstdorf · Kleinwalsertal Bergbahnen ok-bergbahnen.com
The operator's status page: every lift and slope of Fellhorn - Kanzelwand, Nebelhorn and Söllereck with its state, type or difficulty and length. No report time.
Balderschwang skigebiet-balderschwang.de
The ski area's own lift and slope pages: every lift and slope with its state. No report time.
Intermaps snow reports sdds4.intermaps.com
The snow reports the Großer Arber and the Geißkopf embed on their sites: every lift and slope with its state, type or colour, the valley and mountain snow depth and the report time.
German resorts' own status pages
Every lift and slope with its state on the pages of Bad Hindelang - Oberjoch, Wurmberg - Braunlage and Grasgehren (with its snow depths), and every lift with its state on those of Todtnauberg, Kranzberg - Mittenwald, Kreuzberg - Bischofsheim (and its summit station's snow depth) and Münstertal - Wieden: these are open when a lift is. No report time.
Size only: Polish and Austrian areas
Lift and slope totals and km of 12 more Polish areas of over 2 km whose sites show no live states (Master Ski in Tylicz, Henryk Ski, Stożek, Grupa Pingwina's stations and others: their own sites, Małopolska's and Podkarpackie's tourism portals (visitmalopolska.pl), or OpenSkiMap's lifts and runs from OpenStreetMap where they publish no list), and of small areas next to covered ones (Słotwiny Arena, Kompleks Narciarski Słotwiny, Nosal, Füssener Jöchle, Seefeld's Birkenlift; the operators' or tourism sites, OnTheSnow, skiresort.com). Size only: Italian areas
Lift and slope totals and km of 50 Italian areas (Bardonecchia, Roccaraso, Artesina and smaller ones): from OnTheSnow, or the resort's own site; km otherwise skiresort.com's. 13 of them (Roccaraso, Artesina, Piani di Bobbio and smaller ones) are open or closed by their own live list (above); the others have no live status yet.
Size only: French areas
Lift and slope totals and km of 73 French areas whose status we can't read yet (Val Cenis, Saint-Lary, Auron, Les Orres, Chamrousse, Ax 3 Domaines, Orcières and smaller ones): from the resort's own site, otherwise OnTheSnow; km otherwise skiresort.com's. No live status yet.
Size only: Slovak areas slovakia.travel
Lift and slope totals and km of 18 Slovak areas whose status we can't read yet (Martinky, Tále, Roháče - Spálená and smaller ones), and the location of these and of the seven read from their own pages: from each area's page on slovakia.travel, the Slovak tourism agency's site (Vrátna's km too). No live status yet.
Bulgarian resorts' own pages
Every lift and slope with its state on the pages of Bansko (with three snow depths), Borovets (with its snow depth) and Vitosha (online in its season only). No report time.
Size only: Bulgarian areas
Lift totals and km of 9 Bulgarian areas (Pamporovo, Mechi Chal and smaller ones), and Bansko's, Borovets' and Vitosha's km: from skiresort.com. No live status yet.

Lifts and slopes

Resorts' slope maps (Intermaps) intermaps.com
Live status of every lift and slope, with lift types and slope difficulties, for the bigger Austrian areas (the only source for most resorts in Vorarlberg, Styria, Upper and Lower Austria) and for Andermatt Sedrun Disentis, the Friuli Venezia Giulia areas, Madesimo, Schöneben-Haideralm and the German areas with a current map (Winterberg, Postwiese - Neuastenberg, Altastenberg and Sahnehang, which share Winterberg's map and each take their own lift and slope numbers on it, Sudelfeld, Brauneck, Spitzingsee, Großer Arber, Ofterschwang, Bolsterlang, Fichtelberg, Zugspitze, Garmisch-Classic, Feldberg, Hohenbogen). Avoriaz gets its part of the Portes du Soleil's map: the items whose names match its lifts and slopes in OpenSkiData (29 of its 35 lifts, 34 of its 56 slopes).
Resorts' slope maps (Lumiplan) lumiplan.com
Live status of every lift and slope, with lift types, slope difficulties and lengths, for the French resorts whose sites embed a Lumiplan map (e.g. Les 3 Vallées, Paradiski, Évasion Mont-Blanc), split by resort.
Resorts' own websites
The size of most ski areas (total slope km, and lift and slope totals the reports lack), and Hintertux's live open km.
OnTheSnow and skiresort skiinfo.fr skiresort.com
The size of ski areas whose own sites give none: lifts, slopes and km as resorts enter them on OnTheSnow, and skiresort.com's km where neither gives one, and km by difficulty for some bigger areas.
OpenStreetMap contributors openstreetmap.org
Slope lengths for the open km of Kitzsteinhorn and Kaunertal (ODbL).

Avalanche danger

Euregio avalanche bulletin avalanche.report
Avalanche bulletin of the Euregio Tirol – South Tyrol – Trentino, for Tirol, South Tyrol and Trentino resorts.
SLF avalanche bulletin slf.ch
The Swiss avalanche bulletin, for Swiss resorts.
AINEVA bollettini.aineva.it
The joint avalanche bulletin of the Italian regions, for Italian resorts outside South Tyrol and Trentino (those use the Euregio's).
Horská služba ČR horskasluzba.cz
The Czech Mountain Rescue's avalanche bulletin, for Czech resorts in Krkonoše and Jeseníky, read from the EAWS bulletin files that avalanche.report publishes.
Lavínové stredisko HZS laviny.sk
The Slovak avalanche bulletin, for Jasná, Tatranská Lomnica, Štrbské Pleso, Vrátna, Martinky, Malinô Brdo, Jasenská dolina, Roháče - Spálená and Strednica - Ždiar, from the same EAWS files.
TOPR lawiny.topr.pl
The Tatra Volunteer Rescue's avalanche bulletin for the Polish Tatras, for Kasprowy Wierch, from the same EAWS files.
Météo-France meteofrance.com
The French avalanche bulletin (bulletin d'estimation du risque d'avalanche), by massif, for French resorts in the Alps and Pyrenees, from the same EAWS files.
NVE (Varsom) varsom.no
The Norwegian Water Resources and Energy Directorate's avalanche warnings, for Norway's mountain regions.
AEMET, ICGC and the Centre de Lauegi d'Aran aemet.es icgc.cat lauegi.report
Spain's avalanche bulletins, for Spanish resorts in Aragón, Madrid, Cantabria, Catalonia and the Val d'Aran, from the same EAWS files.
Servei Meteorològic Nacional d'Andorra meteo.ad
Andorra's avalanche bulletin, for its three warning regions, from the same EAWS files.
ARSO meteo.arso.gov.si
The Slovenian Environment Agency's avalanche bulletin, for Slovenian resorts in the Julian Alps and Prealps, the Karawanks and the Kamnik and Savinja Alps (not Pohorje), from the same EAWS files.
Lawinenwarndienst Bayern lawinenwarndienst.bayern.de
The Bavarian Avalanche Warning Service's bulletin, for German resorts in the Bavarian Alps, from the same EAWS files.
EAWS warning regions gitlab.com
Region boundaries (CC0), to find each resort's warning region.

Weather

Open-Meteo open-meteo.com
The 7-day forecast on the resort pages, at each resort's bottom and top (CC BY 4.0): the current temperature, weather and wind, and each day's weather, highest and lowest temperature, snowfall, precipitation, strongest gusts and hours of sunshine.

The map (through OpenFreeMap), and the location of the villages of Italian, Czech, Polish and French resorts and of the TMR resorts in Slovakia, of Austrian ones outside the Snow Card and of the Finnish resorts' fells and hills and the German, Spanish and Slovenian ski areas (Nominatim). MapLibre GL JS and maplibre-contour maplibre.org github.com

three.js threejs.org
The lift models on the 3D map in browsers without WebGPU.
Natural Earth naturalearthdata.com
Country borders on the small map at the top of the table page (public domain).

Every source in the current data

Sources with current conditions give the status, lifts, slopes, snow and avalanche danger; sources with resort information give what stays the same, such as the size of the ski area. Each is named by its website, where you'll find the same data. “Credit required” marks a source whose terms ask for a credit; for the others we haven't checked yet, so assume they do.

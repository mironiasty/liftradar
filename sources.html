Snow and resort status

Resorts' own websites
Snow measuring stations of Hintertuxer Gletscher, Sölden, Kitzsteinhorn, Kaunertaler Gletscher and Pitztaler Gletscher.
Tirol Snow Card
Daily reports of the other Tirol resorts (snow depth, lifts, slopes, open/closed), as the resorts submit them to iSKI.
SalzburgerLand snow report
The same kind of report for the other Salzburg resorts, from the regional tourism board (also Planai, Dachstein Gletscher and Feuerkogel).
Kärnten snow report
The same kind of report for the Carinthian resorts, from Kärnten Werbung, the regional tourism board.
Ski Arlberg
Every lift and slope of St. Anton, Lech Zürs and Warth-Schröcken, and the snow depth of St. Anton and Lech Zürs, from the lift companies' live info.
infosnow.ch (APG|SGA)
Live status of Swiss ski areas, as the lift companies publish it: every lift and slope with its type, difficulty and length, plus snow depths where the resort enters them.
SLF, WSL Institute for Snow and Avalanche Research
Measured snow depth and new snow at the stations nearest to Swiss resorts that don't report depths (automatic IMIS stations and observers' study plots), CC BY 4.0.
Open Data Hub South Tyrol
Live status of every lift and slope of the Dolomiti Superski areas, as Dolomiti Superski publishes it (CC0), and the South Tyrol snow report (lifts, slopes, snow measuring points) from the province's tourism database.
Valle d'Aosta snow bulletin
Every lift and slope of the Aosta Valley areas (and Alagna), with snow depth, from the regional tourism board.
Trentino snow bulletin
Lifts, slope km, snow depth and last snowfall of the Trentino areas, from the provincial tourism board.
Valtellina di Bormio
Every lift and slope of Bormio (with San Colombano), Santa Caterina and the Stelvio glacier, from the tourism board.
Livigno
Lift status of Livigno's three lift companies, from the tourism board.
holidayinfo.cz (Sitour CZ)
Snow reports of the Czech ski areas, as the resorts enter them: every lift and slope with its type, difficulty and length, snow depth on the slopes and new snow. CzechTourism's snow report shows the same data.
Polskie Koleje Linowe (PKL)
Every lift and slope of Kasprowy Wierch, Jaworzyna Krynicka, Palenica and Mosorny Groń, with snow cover, from the lift company.
Zieleniec
Every lift and slope of Zieleniec, with snow cover, from Zieleniec Sport Arena. The site refuses the fetcher's servers for now, so only the area's size is shown.
Size only: TMR and other Slovak and Polish areas
Lift and slope totals and km of Jasná, Tatranská Lomnica, Štrbské Pleso, Starý Smokovec and Szczyrk (TMR's slope tables), Kotelnica Białczańska (visitmalopolska.pl), Czarna Góra, Ski Arena Szrenica, Karpacz Ski Arena and Pilsko (their own sites). No live status yet.

Lifts and slopes

Resorts' slope maps (Intermaps)
Live status of every lift and slope, with lift types and slope difficulties, for the bigger Austrian areas (the only source for most resorts in Vorarlberg, Styria, Upper and Lower Austria) and for Andermatt Sedrun Disentis, Engelberg-Titlis, the Friuli Venezia Giulia areas and Madesimo.
Resorts' own websites
The official size of the bigger ski areas (total slope km), and Hintertux's live open km.
OpenStreetMap contributors
Slope lengths for the open km of Kitzsteinhorn and Kaunertal (ODbL).

Avalanche danger

avalanche.report
Avalanche bulletin of the Euregio Tirol – South Tyrol – Trentino, for Tirol, South Tyrol and Trentino resorts.
SLF avalanche bulletin
The Swiss avalanche bulletin, for Swiss resorts.
AINEVA
The joint avalanche bulletin of the Italian regions, for Italian resorts outside South Tyrol and Trentino (those use the Euregio's).
Horská služba ČR
The Czech Mountain Rescue's avalanche bulletin, for Czech resorts in Krkonoše and Jeseníky, read from the EAWS bulletin files that avalanche.report publishes.
Lavínové stredisko HZS
The Slovak avalanche bulletin, for Jasná, Tatranská Lomnica and Štrbské Pleso, from the same EAWS files.
TOPR
The Tatra Volunteer Rescue's avalanche bulletin for the Polish Tatras, for Kasprowy Wierch, from the same EAWS files.
EAWS warning regions
Region boundaries (CC0), to find each resort's warning region.

Map

OpenStreetMap contributors
Map tiles, and the location of the villages of Italian, Czech, Slovak and Polish resorts and of Austrian ones outside the Snow Card (Nominatim). Swiss resorts are placed at their infosnow location.
Leaflet
The interactive map.

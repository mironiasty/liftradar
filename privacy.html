Privacy policy

LiftRadar: the website liftradar.eu and the Android and iOS apps. Effective 17 November 2026.

In short: LiftRadar has no accounts, ads, analytics or cookies, and doesn't collect your personal data. Your favourite resorts and settings stay on your device. To show the data and the maps, your browser or the app downloads files from liftradar.eu and from the services listed below.

Who is responsible

Miron Pawlik, who makes LiftRadar. Contact: miron [at] liftradar.eu.

On your device

  • Your favourite resorts, the language you picked and the map's settings are kept only on your device: in the browser's storage (localStorage) on the website, and in the app's own storage in the apps. They are never sent anywhere.
  • The apps keep a copy of the files they last downloaded, so they open without a signal.
  • All of it goes when you clear the site's data in your browser or uninstall the app.

What your device downloads

Each request tells the server your IP address, details of your browser or app, and what it asks for. For map tiles, that is which part of the map you are looking at. These services follow their own policies:

  • The resort data, the ski maps and the pages: liftradar.eu, served by Cloudflare (Cloudflare's privacy policy).
  • The base map: OpenFreeMap.
  • The land in 3D: elevation tiles from Terrain Tiles on Amazon Web Services (AWS privacy notice), and on the website also from Mapterhorn.
  • On the website, the map's scripts from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr.

Website hosting

Like any web server, Cloudflare sees the IP address and technical details of requests, including those to the API and the MCP server, and makes aggregate traffic statistics from them. We use no cookies and no tracking scripts, and keep no logs of our own.

Phone apps

The apps ask only for internet access. They have no access to your location, contacts, photos or files. The app stores (App Store, Google Play) may collect their own data about installs and crashes under their own terms.

Your rights

We don't keep your personal data, so there is usually nothing to hand over or delete. If you have questions or want to use your rights under the GDPR (access, correction, erasure, objection), write to the address above. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO).

Children

LiftRadar isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
